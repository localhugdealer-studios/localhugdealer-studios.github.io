Prepare to be outnumbered.

Enemy No. 1 is a twin-stick action roguelite set on the number line. It takes its escalating runs from Risk of Rain and its eat, grow and split mechanics from agar.io.

Your number is your health, your size and your damage. Shoot numbers, collect what they drop and watch your number climb. A bigger number hits harder and has more to lose.

You have 1 problem

Numero Uno, in his attempt to become | Absolute |, has caged Zero and corrupted every number with a 1 in it. Get to him, free Zero and put the number line back in balance.

Every pickup changes your number, and any part of you with a 1 in it stops healing.

Split for firepower. Merge to survive.

You are a cluster of nodes, and your primary weapon fires from every one of them. Split and you have more guns on smaller, more fragile bodies. Merge back up when you need to take a hit.

It is also how you deal with a 1. 100 splits into a 55 and a 45, and both of them heal and shoot.

The clock is not on your side

The swarm grows with every minute you spend and every stage you clear. Each stage takes you closer to defeating Numero Uno. Alternatively, continue playing the game in endless mode to challenge yourself against the clock.

A different number every run

55+ stacking pickups

In four rarities. Chain Rule arcs lightning between enemies. Corollary turns kills into homing darts. Inverse Function brings you back from the dead, once.

A dozen+ weapons

Every one a math pun. Primaries like The Common Denominator, Long Division and Pi-stol fire from every node. Secondaries like Null Set, BODMAS and the Factorializer fire from the root, with limited ammo.

10+ arena shapes

Shuffled per run, The Pie Chart, The Unit Circle and The Number Line among them.

Elites with their own tricks

The Unitarians hunt in packs. Primadonnas sprint at you the moment their shield breaks.

Knock the 1 out of an enemy's number and it switches sides

The Square Rootcket does it to everything in its blast.

Find the portal and defeat the stage guardians

Every guardian you beat brings you closer to defeating Numero Uno.

Between runs

Imaginary numbers drop during runs, more often on the harder difficulties, and the Report Card (achievements) pays out more. Spend them on the (skill) Tree Diagram and new weapons.

Also on the syllabus

  • Puzzle sites that pay out artifacts with bigger bonuses
  • The (skill) Tree Diagram, 50+ nodes with free respecs.
  • The Report Card, 40+ achievements to take home.
  • The Log(book), 130+ entries on every weapon, pickup and enemy you meet.
  • A shop called The Absolute Value. Pay the Altar of Operations and its gate is guaranteed.
  • Four difficulties: Rational, Irrational, Imaginary and Undefined.
  • Neon vector art on a CRT, and 10+ custom tracks to go with it.
  • Keyboard and mouse, or gamepad.
  • Stages of about 3 to 5 minutes each. 20 minutes to reach Numero Uno.
